# S.A.F.E House - Design & Brand Identity Guidelines

## 🎨 1. Brand Concept: "Clean, Data-Driven, Environmental"
**S.A.F.E House** (Spatial Analyst for Flood and Environment) mengedepankan keakuratan data spasial.
*   **Persona:** Analitis, Objektif, Bersih, Modern.
*   **Design Goal:** UI yang tidak menghalangi data. Peta harus menjadi fokus utama, sementara elemen UI lain harus terasa ringan (lightweight) dan menyatu.

## 🎨 2. Master Prompt for Google Stitch (Flexible & Clean UI)

**Master Prompt:**
"Design a minimalist, data-focused dashboard for 'S.A.F.E House' (Spatial Analyst for Flood and Environment). The goal is clarity and spatial intelligence. 

**Layout:**
- A clean sidebar for the 'GEO-AI Chat Assistant' on the left.
- A massive, edge-to-edge interactive Satellite Map in the center.
- A discreet layer filter panel on the right.

**Visual Style:**
- Use a 'Modern Scientific' aesthetic. White backgrounds, thin lines, and professional typography (Inter).
- Accent colors: Safe Emerald (#10B981) for safety, Ocean Blue (#3B82F6) for water/flood data, and Slate (#334155) for text.
- Avoid heavy shadows or over-designed elements. Focus on 'Glassmorphism' popups that show a Radar Chart comparing environmental risks (Flood, Soil, Air, Liquefaction).

**Components:**
- Map Pins that are clean and informative.
- Comparison Mode: A side-by-side synchronized map view that highlights differences in flood proximity and soil elevation.
- Micro-interaction: When clicking a point, show a floating summary card with an 'Overall Safety Score'."
