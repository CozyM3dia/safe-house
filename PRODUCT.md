# S.A.F.E House Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

Property buyers, homeowners, builders, and permit or planning teams in Indonesia who need a fast, understandable first-pass risk audit for a specific location. They may not be geophysicists, so the product must translate geospatial and geotechnical evidence into a clear next action while keeping the underlying sources visible.

## Product Purpose

S.A.F.E House turns one map coordinate into a defensible property-risk briefing. It combines government and open geospatial data with deterministic geotechnical calculations to surface flood, liquefaction, soil, seismic, fault, and environmental signals in minutes. The interface succeeds when a user can choose a location, understand the confidence and provenance of the result, and decide whether to investigate further without confusing an early screening with a professional engineering sign-off.

## Brand Personality

Measured, field-ready, and quietly confident. The product should feel like a precision instrument operated by a helpful geoscience analyst: calm under uncertainty, transparent about evidence, and warm enough for a high-stakes property decision.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, glossy AI-chat spectacle, over-decorated card grids, neon cyberpunk map styling, unexplained score theater, and dark interfaces that bury sources in low-contrast microcopy. Do not make the map or the AI feel more authoritative than the underlying evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence before ornament: show where a signal comes from and how certain it is.
2. One calm next step: make selecting a location and starting an audit feel obvious.
3. Spatial context stays visible: interface chrome should frame the map instead of competing with it.
4. Explain the consequence, not just the metric: pair technical values with plain-language meaning.
5. Earn trust through restraint: distinguish official, open-data, proxy, and provisional results without alarmism.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the primary audit flow. Do not rely on color alone for risk state; provide text labels and icons. Maintain visible keyboard focus, logical heading and landmark structure, minimum 44px touch targets on touch devices, readable contrast for secondary text, reduced-motion support, and clear loading, error, and provisional-data states. Indonesian is the primary language, with English available where supported.
